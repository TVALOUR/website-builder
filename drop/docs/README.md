# `drop/docs/`

The paperwork that holds the facts. Menus, price lists, service lists, leaflets,
certificates and registrations, opening hours, an export or PDF print of your old
site, the last quote you sent.

This is where most of the site's real content comes from. Every price, hour,
qualification and claim on the finished site has to trace to something you actually
said or handed over — that is what `facts.md` and the fact gate exist for — and a
leaflet answers a dozen questions in one file.

Scans and phone photos of paper are fine. So is a document that is out of date, as
long as you say which parts are.
