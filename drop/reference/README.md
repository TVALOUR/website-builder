# `drop/reference/`

Sites you love. The one you hate. Screenshots, sketches on paper, a competitor you
want to beat, a page whose layout you want yours to feel like.

The one you *hate* is as useful as the ones you like, and it is the one people
forget to send. "Not like that" removes more wrong answers in one file than three
rounds of adjectives.

Sketches count and are the highest-value thing in this folder. A photo of a boxes
-and-arrows drawing of your home page tells the design stage more about what is in
your head than any amount of describing it.

**Everything here is studied, never shipped.** No layout, image, or line of copy
from a reference gets copied into your site. It informs the direction; it does not
become the material.
