# `drop/photos/`

Your photographs. The premises, the work, the team, the product, the van.

Phone photos are fine. A real, slightly imperfect photograph of your actual shop
beats a perfect stock image of somebody else's, every time — it is the single
biggest difference between a site that is yours and a site that is a template.

Two things to have an answer ready for, because both get asked:

- **Where did it come from?** "I took it", "the photographer we hired in 2023",
  "off our Facebook page" are all real answers.
- **Is it yours to publish?** A photo you paid a photographer for is usually still
  the photographer's copyright unless the contract said otherwise. Photos with
  identifiable people in them need those people to be fine with it.

No photographs at all is a real answer too, and a better one than invented ones.
