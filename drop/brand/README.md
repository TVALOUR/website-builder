# `drop/brand/`

Anything that pins down what your business looks like already.

Brand guidelines as a PDF if you have them. If you do not — and most small
businesses do not — then the hex codes off your old site, a photo of your signage,
a photo of the van, the front of a business card, the paint reference from the
shop front. All of it counts, and all of it beats a colour picked in a vacuum.

If there is genuinely nothing, that is fine and worth saying: the design stage
then chooses a direction with you at a stop, instead of quietly guessing at one.
