## Always

- Ask for photographs at the first meeting, not at stage 04. Every build that went thin on
  imagery went thin because nobody asked early enough to fix it.
- Show two rendered directions before writing a line of real CSS. Adjectives do not survive
  the trip between two people.
- Read the price list back to them out loud before launch. It is the fact they are most
  likely to have changed and least likely to mention.
- When they say "just do whatever you think", ask the three questions that cannot be
  guessed and say that is what you are doing.

## Never

- Never a full-width centred hero with three equal cards under it. It is the shape that
  says nobody decided.
- Never a testimonial without a name, a date and somebody who could confirm it.
- Never a stock photograph of a workshop that is not theirs, however good it looks.
- Never present a direction whose name the pixels do not earn.
