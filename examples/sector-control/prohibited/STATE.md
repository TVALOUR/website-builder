An aesthetics clinic page of the kind every site builder ships: the treatment
named, and priced. Human Medicines Regulations 2012 reg.284(1) prohibits
publishing an advertisement likely to lead to the use of a prescription only
medicine, and botulinum toxin is one.

Nothing else in this repo reads this page as a problem. It is well-formed,
honest about its prices, and unlawful.
