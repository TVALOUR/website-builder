What a survey involves.

Half a day in the building, most of it spent looking at things you cannot see from the outside. You get a written order of works afterwards, priced stage by stage, with the measures that matter most at the top — and no obligation to do any of them with us.

What you keep The order of works is yours whatever you do next. It names the measures, the sequence and the reason for each, so another contractor can price it as easily as we can.

One last thing.

If someone has already quoted you for a heat pump and has not asked what the walls are made of, get a second opinion before you sign it. It does not have to be ours.
