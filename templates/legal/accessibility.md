# Accessibility statement — skeleton

Last reviewed: [DATE]

> **Do not assert bare conformance.** Automated testing fully covers only about
> 57% of issues, and a study of 32 blind users found just 50.4% of the problems
> they actually hit mapped to any success criterion. An unverified public
> conformance claim is a statement of fact nobody has tested.
>
> Say what you aim at, what you did, and what you have NOT done. The honest
> version is more useful to a disabled visitor and safer for the client.

## The standard we aim at

WCAG 2.2 level AA.

## What has been done

[Pull TRUE statements from the gate results — this is the one legal page that can
be written honestly from work actually completed.]

- One h1 per page and headings that step down in order.
- Body text at least 4.5:1 against its background; large text at least 3:1.
  The palette was checked against the ratios, not eyeballed.
- Everything works from the keyboard, and the focus outline is visible rather
  than removed for tidiness.
- A skip link is the first thing you reach, and it goes somewhere real.
- Real landmarks: header, nav, main, footer.
- Reflows to 320px with no sideways scrolling, and to 400% zoom.
- Nothing moves, flashes or plays on its own.
