# Cookie notice — skeleton

Last reviewed: [DATE]

> **READ THIS BEFORE FILLING IT IN. The best cookie banner is no cookie banner.**
>
> If the site loads no analytics, no advertising pixel, no embedded video, no
> embedded map and no third-party font, it sets nothing needing consent. The
> correct output is a page saying exactly that, and **no banner at all**.
>
> A banner on a site with nothing to consent to is a dark pattern with a cost
> and no benefit: it costs every visitor a click, it hurts the metrics it was
> installed to protect, and on a phone it covers the phone number.
>
> Since the DUAA 2025 exceptions came into force on 5 February 2026, cookies
> used solely for first-party statistical purposes by the site operator, and
> those used solely for appearance or functionality, need information and a free
> opt-out rather than prior consent. Genuinely cookieless first-party analytics
> does not engage PECR reg.6 at all.

## What a cookie is

A small file a website asks your browser to keep. Some are needed to make a site
work. Most are for measuring visitors or advertising.
