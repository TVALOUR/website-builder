## Taking them — the phone rules

- **Clean the lens.** More photographs are ruined by a thumbprint than by anything else.
- **Landscape** for anything wide, **portrait** for a person or a tall object. Decide before
  you press, not by rotating it afterwards.
- **Stand still, tap the thing you want sharp**, wait a beat, then press.
- **Flash off.** Always, indoors especially. Open a door or a blind instead.
- **Do not zoom — walk closer.** Phone zoom is a crop and it costs everything.
- **Take five of every shot** from slightly different positions. One of them will be the one.
- **Send the originals.** Not screenshots, and not anything a messaging app has re-compressed
  — those arrive at a fraction of the size the page needs.

## Before you photograph anyone or anything that is not yours

- A **recognisable person** must have agreed to being on a public website. Staff included — a
  staff photograph outlives the job.
- **Somebody else's property, home or premises** needs their agreement too. "Sure, take a
  photo" is not the same as agreeing to it being published.
- **Children:** written permission from a parent, or we do not use it.

## Where to put them

Drop them into `drop/photos/` and tell us. Every photograph gets a row recording where it
came from and who agreed to it — a picture with no row does not go on the site, the same rule
every other fact here follows.
