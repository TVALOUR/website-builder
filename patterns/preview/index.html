The survey is the quote

We measure, look behind things, and write down what we find. You get a fixed price and a list of what would change it, which is usually rot nobody could see and occasionally a wall that is not where the plan says.

Book a survey

What happens after you ring

  1. We talk for ten minutes

    Usually enough to say whether this is a job for us. If it is not, we will say who it is for.

  2. We come and measure

    Free, and it is not a sales visit. If you want to think about it for six months, that is fine.

  3. You get a written price

    Fixed, itemised, and with the things that could change it listed underneath.

  4. We book a date

    Eight to ten weeks out, and we keep it. If we cannot, you hear it from us before you notice.

Questions people actually ask

Do you take on small jobs?

One or two a month, usually for people we have worked for before. Ring and ask. If we say no we will tell you who to try.

Why is the lead time so long?

Because there are two of us and we do not run more than one job at a time. It is the reason the work is what it is, and it is the honest trade.

Do you want money up front?

A third when we order materials, a third on delivery to site, the rest when you are happy. Nothing before the survey.

Finding the workshop

Trescott Yard is the third turning off the Bickington road after the garage, signed for the industrial units. We are the long grey shed at the back, past the skip hire.

If your satnav sends you to Trescott Farm, you have gone half a mile too far. Turn round at the chapel.

Getting in

Park anywhere on the concrete. The workshop door is the one that is open. Level access throughout, no step at the threshold, and the toilet is accessible.

Dogs are fine outside. Not in the machine shop.
