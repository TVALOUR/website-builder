Finding the workshop

Trescott Yard is the third turning off the Bickington road after the garage, signed for the industrial units. We are the long grey shed at the back, past the skip hire.

If your satnav sends you to Trescott Farm, you have gone half a mile too far. Turn round at the chapel.

Getting in

Park anywhere on the concrete. The workshop door is the one that is open. Level access throughout, no step at the threshold, and the toilet is accessible.

Dogs are fine outside. Not in the machine shop.
